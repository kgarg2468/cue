M1 process-group termination slice verification — 2026-07-18

(Command::process_group(0)), and all five kill sites in run_process

failures) now SIGKILL the whole group via kill_process_group() with a
direct child.kill() fallback, so shell-spawned descendants cannot
survive cancellation or timeout. libc 0.2 added to backend deps.

Independent review (Codex gpt-5.6-sol, high effort): no blockers; three
major and two minor findings. Dispositions after code verification:
- FIXED: natural leader exit skipped the group kill entirely (Exited
  precedence), so `sh -c 'sleep 30 & exit 0'` leaked the descendant —
  even after an accepted cancel. The Exited branch now kills the process
  group before emitting the terminal frame; covered by the new
  natural_leader_exit_kills_descendant_processes test (red-verified
  against the unfixed branch) and runtime-demo-natural-exit.log (exit_code
  0, grandchild dead 0.00s after run_exit with ~30s remaining).
- FIXED: test cleanup SIGKILLed a raw pid after 5s (pid-reuse hazard) and
  had no panic guard. Replaced with DescendantGuard: Drop-based cleanup
  that verifies via ps that the pid still runs the test's `sleep 30`
  before signalling, active on every exit path including panics.
- FIXED: 500 ms run timeout left too little margin for spawn/pid-parse on
  loaded runners, and read_grandchild_pid panicked opaquely on EOF or a
  premature terminal. Timeout raised to 2 s with a 10 s client read
  timeout (a first fix attempt set both to 2 s, which deterministically
  raced — caught by 8x stress reruns); the pid reader now fails with
  explicit messages on EOF or run_exit-before-pid.
- FLAGGED (follow-up M1 backend-lifecycle slice): process_group(0) means
  dev-terminal Ctrl-C signals the backend but no longer its runs, and
  backend shutdown does not yet terminate active run groups; run stdin
  also remains inherited pending waiting-input work.
- FLAGGED (inherent, documented): kill(-pgid) after the leader is reaped
  has a theoretical pgid-reuse window (shared with the pre-existing
  cancel path); eliminating it needs leader-zombie retention or
  kqueue-based tracking, out of scope here.
