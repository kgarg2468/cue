M1 pause/resume slice verification — 2026-07-18 (cycle 9)

Slice: versioned pause_process/resume_process requests over the Unix socket
protocol (spec adapter API: pause(runId)/resume(runId), spec lines 603-604;
one-click pause, line 865). Pause SIGSTOPs the run's whole process group,
resume SIGCONTs it; accepted/not_found is existence-based like cancel. The
registry value became RunControl {cancelled, paused, pgid}; the supervision
loop stores the pgid post-spawn and a SeqCst store-then-load handshake on
(paused, pgid) ensures a pause landing in the register-to-spawn window is
applied by exactly one side. The timeout deadline deliberately keeps ticking
while paused, and cancel/timeout/shutdown SIGKILL a stopped group unchanged.
Swift IPCClient gains pauseProcess/resumeProcess mirroring cancelProcess.
